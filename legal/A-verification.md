## C. Publishing personal data, severing identifiers, access to information

### C1. *Orange Democratic Movement Party (ODM) v IEBC* (Petition 440 of 2019) [2019] KEHC 2492 (KLR), Makau J, 6 Nov 2019 — CORRECTED (material) → second pass: CORRECTED further (telephone numbers withheld)
- **What the report said:** the court ordered the Kibra register released "minus personal details" (ID/passport numbers) while names were disclosed, and the memo used this as the leading authority for severing identifiers.
- **What the judgment says (KL snippets, `src/klq_odm_*.json`):**
  - IEBC's position: "It claims, that it provided redacted details of the identification numbers of the ... details of the identification numbers of the voters in order to protect the confidentiality"; "voters in order to protect the confidentiality of their personal details urging, ... of their personal details and the right of the voter to privacy of their personal".
  - ODM's position: the register "contain the names of the voters, identification numbers (truncated), polling station, number and voter registration number"; "incomplete … for all purposes and intentions".
  - Finding on privacy: "**The Respondent has not demonstrated how Article 31 of the Constitution of Kenya is** ..." [infringed].
  - Finding on IDs: "…production of I/D Card or passport and recording his name, I/D or passport number and even telephone number. ... **I find for free and fair election to be had, national I/D or passport numbers provide** ..."; "The particulars required to be supplied in Form A is the National I/D or passport".
  - Result: "In view of my findings herein above, I find and hold that this petition succeeds."; order "(c) An order of mandamus be and is **HEREBY issued compelling the Respondent to publish the Kibra Constituency voters' register containing the names, National ID and Passport numbers and their** ..." [polling stations]; costs: "...its own costs".
- **Corrected reading:** the High Court rejected IEBC's privacy objection and ordered publication of the register **with** national ID/passport numbers. The "minus personal details" line came from one press report; *The Standard* (6 Nov 2019, `src/std_kibra.html`) wrote "release the Kibra voters register safe for confidential information", and Citizen's reports conflict. ODM v IEBC is therefore authority **against**, not for, severing ID numbers from an electoral record requested by a party under Art. 35. The recommendation to mask IDs on published result forms must rest on the DPA itself (ss.25(d), 41, 51(1)), ATI s.6(1)(d), and Art. 24(1)(e), not on this case.
- URLs: `https://kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2019/2492/eng@2019-11-06` (via search API); `https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/nairobi/article/2001348370/iebc-ordered-to-release-kibra-voters-register`; `https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2019-10-31-odm-says-kibra-voter-register-incomplete-demands-id-numbers/`.
- **Second pass — judgment read in full** (`https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2019/2492/eng@2019-11-06`, `src2/j_odm_2019.txt`): the ID-number reading above is CONFIRMED, but it is **CORRECTED as incomplete on telephone numbers**. ODM's alternative prayer asked for a register "containing the names, National ID and Passport numbers, **telephone numbers** and their respective polling stations" (para 1(c)). The court granted names and ID/passport numbers and expressly withheld telephone numbers:
  - Para 48: "The Respondent has not demonstrated how Article 31 of the Constitution of Kenya is violated by giving the voter's name and his/her ID/Passport number."
  - Para 49: "I find for free and fair election to be had, national I/D or passport numbers provide the only unique means of identifying individual … that a complete register containing all the prescribed information in the Form A … (**save confidential information and which may be injurious to voters such as telephone numbers**, home or property searched, possessions seized and information relating to their family or private affairs unnecessarily required or revealed or privacy of their communication which should not be infringed) be made public".
  - Para 53, orders: "(b) An order be and is Hereby issued compelling the Respondent to provide … Kibra Constituency voters' register containing all information prescribed in Form A … **save confidential information and which may be injurious to voters such as telephone numbers** … which is protected under Article 31 of the Constitution of Kenya 2010; (c) An order of mandamus … compelling the Respondent to publish the Kibra Constituency voters' register containing the names, National ID and Passport numbers, and their respective polling stations and centers …" (prayer (c)'s telephone numbers left out).
  - Para 52: opening the register for inspection "is not to open register for members of public to check on details or particulars of other voters".
- **Corrected reading (second pass):** *ODM v IEBC* is authority **against** severing ID/passport numbers from a voters' register sought under Art. 35, and authority **for** withholding telephone numbers from a published electoral record as "confidential information … which may be injurious" and protected by Art. 31. *The Standard*'s "safe for confidential information" describes order (b) accurately. For result forms: the case does not support masking ID numbers; it does support masking phone numbers.
