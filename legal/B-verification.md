## 1. What the prescribed forms legally require (Forms 34A, 34B, 34C)

### 1.1 Which instrument prescribes the current forms — CORRECTED
- Report said (§0.3 item 3, §3.1.11, §6, §8.1, §8.3(3), §8.4): the 2012 Form 34 had no ID or telephone fields; whether the ID/telephone columns on the 2017 and 2022 printed forms are prescribed by the Schedule "is UNVERIFIED".
- Finding: the **Elections (General) (Amendment) Regulations, 2017, Legal Notice No. 72 of 2017** (Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 65, Legislative Supplement No. 31, 21 April 2017) replaced the whole Schedule. Regulation 44: "The principal Regulations are amended by deleting the Schedule and substituting therefor the following new Schedule—". The new **Form 34A (r. 79(2)(a))** contains, after the presiding officer and deputy presiding officer lines, a table headed "Agents or Candidates (if present)" with the columns **"No. | Name of Candidate or Agent | ID/Passport No. | Party Name/Independent Candidate | Tel. Contact | Signature | Date"**, followed by "Reasons for Refusal to Sign (if any)" and "Presiding Officer's Comments". There is **no stamp field** on Form 34A or 34B (Form 34D, the President-elect's certificate, has "Stamp").
- Verbatim (gazetted LN 72/2017, Schedule, p. 423): "Presiding Officer: ………Signature…… Date..... / Deputy Presiding Officer: ………Signature …… Date...... / Agents or Candidates (if present) / No. Name of Candidate or Agent | ID/Passport No. | Party Name/Independent Candidate | Tel. Contact | Signature | Date / 1. 2. 3. / Reasons for Refusal to Sign (if any)".
- **Form 34B (r. 87(1)(a))**: same agents table ("ID/Passport No." and "Tel. Contact"), then "Name of the Constituency Returning Officer / ID Number / Signature / Date", then a "Handing over – Taking over at the National Presidential Tallying Centre" block with the CRO's and the "Commission Chairperson"'s "ID Number", signature, date and time, and "Number of FORM 34 A submitted / received".
- **Form 34C (r. 87(3)(b))**: "Signatures of Candidates or Agents" table with the same "ID/Passport No." and "Tel. Contact" columns, and "Commission Chairperson / ID Number / Signature / Date".
- Sources read: gazetted LN 72/2017 as hosted by the IEBC, https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/o8K6y0Co5S.pdf (`src/B_ln72-2017-iebc.pdf`, pp. 365, 422–424); Kenya Law "Rev. 2017" consolidation of the Elections Act subsidiary legislation hosted by the IEBC, https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/902SFZiDqt.pdf (`src/B_iebc-subsidiary-legislation-compilation.pdf`, Schedule pp. 145–148, note "[L.N. 72/2017, r. 44.]").
- The printed 2022 Form 34A (see item 6.3) reproduces this layout with 11 agent rows and 11 candidate rows, an IEBC logo, a QR code and a pre-printed serial number; the Schedule shows three illustrative rows. Nothing in LN 72/2017 or any later instrument prescribes the number of rows, the QR code or the serial number.
- **Second pass — CONFIRMED on Kenya Law's current consolidation** (`https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/ln/2012/128`, "This is the latest version of this Legal Notice", 31 Dec 2022; `src2/ln128_2012.txt`). Its only amendment is "[Amended by Elections (General) (Amendment) Regulations, 2017 (Legal Notice 72 of 2017) on 5 May 2017]". "FORM 34A (r. 79(2)(a))" carries "Agents or Candidates (if any)" (Kenya Law's wording; the Gazette has "(if present)") with "No. | Name of Candidate or Agent | ID/Passport No. | Party Name/Independent Candidate | Tel. Contact | Signature | Date". The ID and telephone columns are prescribed and no later instrument removes them.

## 2. The duty to publish: Elections Act s.39 as it stands

### 2.1 Text of s.39 — CONFIRMED, with CORRECTED sub-section numbering
- Read in the Kenya Law **Revised Edition 2019** of the Elections Act hosted by the IEBC (https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/G16qxyy4rA.pdf, `src/B_iebc-elections-act-2019.pdf`, pp. 24–25, annotation "[Act No. 36 of 2016, s. 14, Act No. 34 of 2017, s. 6.]"). The same text is reprinted in the annex to the Elections (Amendment) (No. 2) Bill, 2024 (Parliament PDF, pp. 577–578).
- Verbatim:
  > (1C) For purposes of a presidential election, the Commission shall — (a) electronically transmit and physically deliver the tabulated results of an election for the President from a polling station to the constituency tallying centre and to the national tallying centre; (b) tally and verify the results received at the constituency tallying centre and the national tallying centre; and (c) publish the polling result forms on an online public portal maintained by the Commission.
  > (1D) The Commission shall verify that the results transmitted under this section are an accurate record of the results tallied, verified and declared at the respective polling stations.
  > (1E) Where there is a discrepancy between the electronically transmitted and the physically delivered results, the Commission shall verify the results and the result which is an accurate record of the results tallied, verified and declared at the respective polling station shall prevail.
  > (1F) Any failure to transmit or publish the election results in an electronic format shall not invalidate the result as announced and declared by the respective presiding and returning officers at the polling station and constituency tallying centre, respectively.
  > (1G) The Commission shall, to facilitate public information, establish a mechanism for the livestreaming of results as announced at polling stations, and the results so streamed shall be for purposes of public information only and shall not be the basis for a declaration by the Commission.
  > (1H) The chairperson of the Commission shall declare the results of the election of the President in accordance with Article 138(10) of the Constitution.
  > (2) The Chairperson may declare a candidate elected as the President before all the constituencies have transmitted their results if the Commission is satisfied the results that have not been received will not affect the result of the election.
  > (3) The Commission shall announce the final results in the order in which the tallying of the results is completed.
- Correction: the report described (1D) as the discrepancy rule, (1E) as the failure-to-transmit rule, (1F) as "definitional" and (1G) as live-streaming. The enacted numbering is (1D) accurate-record verification, (1E) discrepancy, (1F) failure to transmit or publish, (1G) live-streaming, (1H) chairperson's declaration; and (2)–(3) were also rewritten in 2017 (the 2011 "provisional results" wording is gone). (1A) and (1B) are as quoted above in the 2019 edition and match s.14 of the Election Laws (Amendment) Act, 2016.
- **Second pass — CONFIRMED on Kenya Law's current text** (`https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2011/24`, "This is the latest version of this Act", 31 Dec 2022; `src2/elections_act.txt`). s.39(1)–(3) read as quoted above (Kenya Law spells "live-streaming" in (1G) and capitalises "Chairperson" in (1H)), annotated "[Act No. 36 of 2016, s. 14, Act No. 34 of 2017, s. 6.]", with no note of the *Katiba* declarations. The history's last entries are "[Amended by Political Parties (Amendment) Act, 2022 (Act No. 2 of 2022) on 11 February 2022]" and "[Revised by 24th Annual Supplement (Legal Notice 221 of 2023) on 31 December 2022]", so no Elections Act amendment of 2023–2026 is recorded. s.2 "biometric" ("… DNA, and signatures") and "agent", s.44A and s.83 (2017 text, "[Act No. 34 of 2017, s. 9.]") also match the Rev. 2019 edition.
- 2016 wording CONFIRMED from the gazetted Election Laws (Amendment) Act, 2016 (No. 36 of 2016), s.14 (https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/rPm78XBbWF.pdf, `src/B_election-laws-amendment-act-2016.pdf`, pp. 916–917): "(1C) ... (a) electronically transmit, in the prescribed form, the tabulated results ...; (b) tally and verify the results received at the national tallying centre; and (c) publish the polling result forms on an online public portal maintained by the Commission. (1D) The chairperson of the Commission shall declare the results ...". Original 2011 s.39(1)–(3) CONFIRMED from the Rev. 2016 edition (https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/kqI5cmgeyB.pdf): "(2) Before determining and declaring the final results of an election under subsection (1), the Commission may announce the provisional results of an election. (3) The Commission shall announce the provisional and final results in the order in which the tallying of the results is completed."

## 3. What courts treat as proof that a form is authentic

### 3.1 *Raila Odinga & another v IEBC & 2 others*, Presidential Petition No. 1 of 2017, judgment of 20 September 2017 — CONFIRMED from the Supreme Court's PDF, with CORRECTED figures and quotations
- Source: https://supremecourt.judiciary.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Raila-Amolo-Odinga-v-Independent-Electoral-and-Boundaries-Commission-Supreme-Court-Presidential-Petition-No.-1-of-2017-Judgment-20th-september-2017.pdf (`src/B_odinga-2017-judgment-sc.pdf`, "DATED and DELIVERED at NAIROBI this 20th Day of September 2017", coram Maraga CJ, Mwilu DCJ, Ojwang, Wanjala, Njoki, Lenaola SCJJ; "[405] By a majority of four (with J. B. Ojwang and N. S. Ndungu, SCJJ dissenting)"). The Kenya Law neutral citation [2017] KESC 42 could not be checked.
- Registrar's report (paras 343–350): the IEBC produced "1 Form 34C, 291 Forms 34B, and 41,451 Forms 34As"; the Registrar observed that some forms "appeared to be carbon copies", "appeared to be photocopies" and "had no evidence of being stamped or signed". The checklist (para 344): "the presence of a watermark using the UV reader; colour of the forms; serialization; Microtext; X10 magnification; Column for comments on the form; Format of the forms; and Anti-copy". Forms 34B (para 347): "out of the 291 Forms 34B scrutinized 56 forms bore no watermark, 5 forms had not been signed by the returning officer, 31 forms had no serial numbers, 32 forms had not been signed by the respective party agents, the 'hand over' section of 189 forms had not been filled and the 'take over' section of 287 forms had not been filled."
