# Legal research: data protection and the publication of election results in Kenya

Research done in October 2026 to decide how the redactor and the harness
should behave under Kenyan law. Each claim carries its source and how far it
was checked against the primary text (CONFIRMED, CORRECTED, STILL
UNVERIFIED). It is research to inform decisions, not legal advice: the open
points in `C-recommendations.md` §4 are for a Kenyan lawyer.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `A-data-protection.md` | Privacy and data protection: Constitution Art. 31, Data Protection Act 2019, the 2021 regulations (reg. 26 localisation), ODPC guidance and determinations, case law |
| `A-verification.md` | A's unverified points re-checked against primary sources |
| `B-electoral-framework.md` | The electoral framework for results forms: Constitution Arts. 81, 86, Elections Act s.39, Elections (General) Regulations 2012 and the prescribed forms, IEBC practice, the 2017 and 2022 petitions |
| `B-verification.md` | B's unverified points re-checked against primary sources |
| `C-recommendations.md` | Where the two meet, 18 areas with options for the redactor and the harness, a decision table, and open legal points |

The sources downloaded while checking (including a published 2022 Form 34A
showing agents' details) are not kept here.
