# Legal research: data protection and the publication of election results in Kenya

Research done in October 2026 to decide how the redactor and the harness
should behave under Kenyan law. Each claim carries its source and how far it
was checked against the primary text (CONFIRMED, CORRECTED, STILL
UNVERIFIED). It is research to inform decisions, not legal advice: the open
points in `C-recommendations.md` §4 are for a Kenyan lawyer.

## Changes, 31 October 2026 (second verification pass)

Kenya Law's document pages became readable in full (a headless browser
completes its JavaScript check); the ODPC website was still under
maintenance, so every ODPC document is still unverified. Each item is logged
under "Second pass" in `A-verification.md` and `B-verification.md`; the
reports and `C-recommendations.md` were corrected where they relied on it.

- Confirmed from full text: DPA s.25(d), s.31(5) (60 days), s.43 (72 hours),
  s.49(1), s.51(1); reg. 26 and reg. 2 of LN 263; Elections Act s.2
  ("biometric … and signatures") and s.39(1C)(c); the Form 34A
  "ID/Passport No." and "Tel. Contact" columns (LN 128 of 2012, only
  amendment LN 72 of 2017).
- *ODM v IEBC* (Kibra) withheld voters' telephone numbers as confidential
  under Art. 31 while ordering ID numbers published: adverse for masking IDs,
  supportive for masking phones.
- Reg. 37(1)(a) deems real risk of harm only where a name or ID number comes
  with Second Schedule data (financial, health and similar), which result
  forms do not carry: a form leak needs a case-by-case s.43(1) assessment.
- Reg. 41(2) transfer records attach to safeguards-based transfers and are
  "provided to the Commissioner on request".
- s.37 (commercial use): the statutory-authority limb also requires notice to
  the data subject at collection.
- The ATI (General) Regulations 2023 (LN 161 of 2023) expressly provide for
  redaction; ATI s.28(10) penalises concealing part of disclosed information
  "with intent to deceive".
- *Odhiambo* (2026), para 95(2): a registered mobile number is a digital
  identifier protected by Art. 31(c)–(d). LN 163 of 2015 (SIM registration)
  is revoked by LN 90 of 2025.
- Registration of Persons Act s.14(1)(k)–(m) has confidentiality offences
  (protecting the register, not a number written on a form).
- *Katiba* (Court of Appeal, 2023) upheld the invalidity of the 2017
  s.39(1C)(a), (1E), (1F), (1G); (1D) stands; (1C)(c) untouched.
- 26 September 2022 Supreme Court judgment read: no "digital signatures" in
  the Court's list of security features; "photoshop gone wrong" is not in it.
- Case details corrected: *Tumaz*, *Gachoka*, *Rajput*, *Ndung'u v Wachira*,
  *Mue*, *Regus* dates, Ndung'u SCJ's 2017 dissent, *Ojuok v Okal* (Election
  Appeal 3 of 2018), *Kaloki*, *KHRC v Chebukati*; *Nduati v Chebukati* is not
  an electoral case.
